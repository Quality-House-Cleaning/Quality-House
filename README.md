# Quality House Cleaning

Custom website and digital presence developed from the ground up for **Quality House Cleaning**, a locally owned residential cleaning business serving Palmer Lake, Monument, Tri-Lakes, and surrounding areas in Colorado.

**Live website:** https://quality-housecleaning.com/

## Project Overview

The goal of this project was to create a polished, trustworthy, mobile-friendly online presence that helps a local service business turn visitors into estimate requests while building a strong foundation for local search visibility.

The project covers the complete process from visual design and front-end development through deployment, custom domain configuration, lead capture, search-engine setup, social integration, and supporting print/marketing materials.

## Features

- Custom responsive website
- Mobile, tablet, and desktop layouts
- Service and pricing/booking sections
- Free online estimate form
- Estimate requests delivered directly to the business
- Click-to-call and email contact actions
- Facebook, Instagram, and Google Business Profile integration
- Custom domain with HTTPS
- Local SEO-focused page structure and metadata
- Google Search Console setup and indexing
- XML sitemap and search-engine crawl configuration
- LocalBusiness / HouseCleaning structured data
- Open Graph metadata for social sharing
- Optimized image assets and responsive presentation
- Accessible semantic HTML and navigation
- GitHub-based version control and deployment

## Local SEO

The site was structured around the business's primary service area, including **Palmer Lake, Monument, and the Tri-Lakes area of Colorado**.

SEO implementation includes:

- Search-focused title and meta description
- Canonical URL
- Robots directives
- Geographic metadata
- Semantic headings and service content
- LocalBusiness structured data
- Service-area information
- Google Business Profile connection
- Google Search Console verification
- Sitemap submission and indexing setup

## Lead Generation

A dedicated **Free Online Estimate** form collects:

- Customer name
- Phone number
- Email
- Preferred contact method
- Bedrooms and bathrooms
- Approximate square footage
- Service type
- Preferred date/time
- Service address/area
- Additional cleaning specifications

Requests are routed directly to Quality House Cleaning.

## Supporting Brand & Marketing Work

The digital project was complemented with offline and social marketing assets:

- Facebook promotional content
- Minimalist social-media advertisement
- Google Review cards designed for post-service customer follow-up
- QR-enabled business cards linking directly to the website
- Print-ready layouts for physical marketing materials
- Coordinated website, social media, Google profile, and printed touchpoints

The Google Review cards are intended to be included with small customer thank-you packages after service, creating a simple bridge from the in-home experience to online reputation building.

## Technology

- HTML5

## Design Direction

The visual system was created around a clean, premium residential-service aesthetic: restrained typography, generous spacing, soft neutral surfaces, strong calls to action, and imagery that communicates a calm, spotless home.

The website experience was designed to reinforce three core signals:

**Trust · Local Service · Professional Quality**

## Business Results the Project Supports

This implementation gives the business infrastructure to:

- Receive estimate leads online
- Establish a professional branded web presence
- Be indexed by Google
- Strengthen local search visibility
- Connect website visitors with social profiles
- Build Google reviews through physical follow-up materials
- Convert printed business cards into website traffic using QR codes
- Maintain and improve the website through version control
